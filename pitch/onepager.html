It follows the rules

Most of what is sold in this category does not. That is worth two minutes of your time before you buy anything like it, from me or anyone.

  • No filter screen. The tag opens Google directly. It never asks “how was your experience?” first to route unhappy guests somewhere private. That is review gating — banned by Google, and covered by the FTC rule in force since October 2024.
  • No incentives. Nothing is ever offered in exchange for a review. Google removes those reviews and can suspend the whole profile.
  • Yelp handled correctly. Yelp does not allow businesses to ask for reviews, and the penalty lands on you, not on the vendor. So that end says “Find us on Yelp” and opens your Yelp page, not a review form. That is the wording Yelp itself hands out.
