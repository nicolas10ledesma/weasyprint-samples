  • The second zone is yours. Google always holds one. The other goes to Yelp, TripAdvisor, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, your Booking or Expedia listing, your website or your menu — whichever is worth more to you.

It follows the rules

  • No filter screen. The tag opens Google directly. It never asks “how was your experience?” first to route unhappy guests somewhere private. That is review gating — banned by Google, and covered by the FTC rule in force since October 2024.
  • No incentives. Nothing is ever offered in exchange for a review. Google removes those reviews and can suspend the whole profile.
  • Yelp handled correctly. Yelp does not allow businesses to ask for reviews, and the penalty lands on you, not the vendor. So that end says “Find us on Yelp” and opens your Yelp page, not a review form.
